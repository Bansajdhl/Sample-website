Computer Game

Counter Strike

Tactical, team-based shooter where players compete to complete objectives, such as planting or defusing a bomb.

Introduction

Counter-Strike is a legendary series of tactical first-person shooters where two teams—the Terrorists and the Counter-Terrorists—battle over objective-based rounds. Originally launched in 1999 as a fan-made mod for Half-Life, it quickly became a global phenomenon, defining the "search and destroy" genre through its high-stakes gunplay and focus on team coordination.

  • Success in the game isn't just about fast reflexes; it requires mastering map strategy, managing a complex in-game economy to buy weapons, and executing precise utility like smoke and flashbang grenades.
  • Today, through titles like CS:GO and Counter-Strike 2, it stands as one of the most popular and influential pillars of the competitive esports world.

How to play this game

  1. To play Counter-Strike, you join either the Terrorist or Counter-Terrorist team and engage in round-based combat where the primary goal is to either eliminate the entire enemy team or complete specific map objectives, such as planting or defusing a bomb.
  2. You start each round by using currency earned from previous performances to purchase firearms, armor, and tactical utility like grenades.
  3. Success requires mastering "recoil control" for various weapons, communicating with teammates to execute strategic maneuvers, and using environmental sound cues to track enemy movements, all while managing the high-pressure stakes of having only one life per round.
